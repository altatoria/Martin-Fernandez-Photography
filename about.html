About Me

About Me

Every photograph tells a story, and I've spent the last 12 years capturing some of the most inspiring ones. My journey has taken me from the electrifying world of extreme sports, where I've captured the determination in an athlete's eyes, to the serene beauty of remote landscapes, showcasing the grandeur of nature in its purest form.

Driven by curiosity and creativity, my work reflects a passion for exploring the extraordinary. I've had the privilege of collaborating with global brands such as The North Face, Nike, Patagonia, Quiksilver, Roxy, Under Armour, and DC Shoes. Additionally, I've been a brand ambassador for GoPro and RedBull, where I brought their adventurous spirit to life through compelling visuals. My photography has been featured in exhibitions and published in renowned platforms, sharing unique perspectives with diverse audiences.

Beyond commercial projects, I've supported various environmental initiatives and foundations, using photography to raise awareness and promote the protection of our planet. I've also had the honor of working with world-class athletes like Tony Hawk and Memphis Depay, capturing their achievements and the energy of their craft.

While photography is no longer my full-time profession, it remains my greatest passion. Through the lens, I continue to share the stories that inspire me and celebrate the beauty of the world around us.

I invite you to explore my portfolio and join me in this ongoing journey of creativity and discovery.
