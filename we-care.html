Emergency Relief

When Disaster Strikes,
Awesome People Show Up

Over a decade ago, a major earthquake struck Chile — and that became the first mobilization. Since then, the network has been activated again and again: wildfires in Valparaíso, Matanzas, Puertecillo, and Zapallar; floods across central Chile. Each time, the response is the same — show up with what's needed.

Masks to breathe through smoke. Steel-toe shoes for clearing rubble. Wheelbarrows. Clean drinking water. Warm blankets. The needs vary, but the commitment doesn't.

Photography has a unique power to document, amplify, and connect — to turn distance into solidarity. These initiatives use that power to raise funds and awareness where it matters most.

Your donation becomes action.

Mentorship

Vivi & Raúl
Imprenta Kairos

Behind every small business is a story of courage and persistence. Co-Emprende is a mentorship program that walks alongside small business founders — not to hand them answers, but to help them find their own.

Working with Vivi and Raúl at Imprenta Kairos, the mentorship went beyond business planning. It was about confidence, clarity, and building something sustainable. The result: a feature on Chile's TVN television, and a business with a stronger foundation than ever.

The work continues with new founders who deserve the same chance to be seen and supported.

Creative Empowerment

Empowering
Creators

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't. This program supports aspiring photographers from under-resourced communities — giving them not just the technical skills, but the business knowledge to build a sustainable creative career.

Participants receive training in the full picture: pricing, client management, accounting, taxes. Because being a great photographer isn't enough — you also have to know how to run a business.

The goal is simple: remove the barriers that keep talented people from doing the work they were meant to do.

This initiative is fully independent. No donated funds from emergency relief efforts are used for this program.
